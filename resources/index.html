How to recover this transfer
  1. Sender selects the same files or folder and creates a new code.
  2. Receiver enters that code, chooses Recover, and picks the same destination folder.
  3. Both compare the new words and approve. Matching completed files are checked and reused; incomplete files restart.

Recovery rereads both copies and needs destination-folder access. Already-created files and folders may remain.
